The Duel

You and the Thorn Warden duel with lantern cards. Each turn you gain one more lantern-light (mana, up to 6), refill it, and draw a card.

Play cards by clicking them or pressing 1–5. Their light pays the cost shown in the blue gem. End Turn (Space or the button) when you are done.

Read the Warden: her next move is always shown above her. Ward against her strikes, strike through her wards, and guard your own life.

  • Bloom ✿ heals and grows
  • Frost ❄ chills the Warden's power
  • Ember ✹ strikes; embers chain for more
  • Shade ✦ meddles with her light
